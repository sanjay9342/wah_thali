1. Quick Start

This guide explains how to operate the Wah Thali website after launch. The admin panel is used for live restaurant operations, order handling, product control, coupons, customers, settings, and staff permissions.

Admin URL: https://wahthali.in/admin
Temporary hosting note: the website is currently arranged for temporary Netlify hosting while the GoDaddy domain or hosting issue is resolved.

Daily Opening Routine

  1. Open the admin dashboard.
  2. Check store mode in Settings: Open, Busy, Paused, or Closed.
  3. Review low-stock products in Dashboard and Inventory.
  4. Open Orders page and keep it visible during business hours.
  5. Confirm payment, WhatsApp alerts, and desktop order sound are working.

Daily Closing Routine

  1. Complete pending live orders.
  2. Mark delivered or cancelled orders correctly.
  3. Pause or close ordering if the kitchen is closed.
  4. Review sales, customer issues, refunds, and low-stock products.
  5. Do not share admin login details outside authorized staff.

2. Admin Roles and Access

Role Use Case Access Level
Admin Owner or senior person controlling the full website. Dashboard, orders, inventory, categories, coupons, customers, settings, staff access.
Manager Restaurant operations manager. Full operational access, including settings and staff role management.
Staff Kitchen or order handling staff. Orders only. Used for accepting, declining, preparing, dispatching, and delivery updates.

Only trusted users should receive Admin or Manager access.

4. Dashboard Guide

The dashboard gives a quick overview of restaurant operations. It is best used at the beginning of the day and during peak order hours.

  • Today's sales: shows sales and net revenue for the current day.
  • Orders: shows total orders and how many are currently live.
  • Customers: shows customer activity and repeat customer count.
  • Active products: shows how many menu products are available.
  • Action queue: shows low-stock or urgent operational alerts.
  • Product availability: quickly hide or show a product on the customer menu.

Incoming Orders

  • Use desktop alerts and order sound during working hours.
  • New orders are highlighted on the page.
  • Check payment summary before preparing food.
  • Use the status dropdown or quick action buttons to update progress.

Order Tools

  • Print bill: prints a thermal-style restaurant bill.
  • Message: opens WhatsApp message to customer.
  • ETA: adds custom expected time for customer tracking.
  • Rider note: saves rider, area, or location note.

Reset Customer Orders

The Orders page includes an admin-only reset button at the bottom. This deletes all order history, payments, order items, timelines, and reviews from the website while keeping customer accounts saved.

Use this only before final launch or when test data must be removed. It requires a confirmation checkbox and typing RESET ORDERS. This cannot be undone from the website.

6. Inventory and Products

Use Inventory to manage all customer-facing dishes and add-ons.

  • Add or edit product name, category, description, price, original price, GST rate, prep time, rating, bestseller flag, spice level, and availability.
  • Upload or set product image.
  • Control stock and reorder level.
  • Add variants such as regular, large, half, full, etc.
  • Add extra add-ons such as egg, chicken, paneer, extra rice, etc.
  • If a product is unavailable, mark it unavailable instead of deleting it.
Recommended practice: update product availability before peak hours so customers do not order unavailable items.

7. Categories

  • Create and rename categories.
  • Set category visibility.
  • Control sort order.
  • Upload category image.
  • Add category offer text for menu display.

8. Coupons

  • Create coupon code and label.
  • Choose fixed amount or percentage discount.
  • Set minimum order value and maximum discount.
  • Set start and end dates.
  • Use VIP or loyalty points eligibility where required.

Coupon Handling Rules

DoAvoid
Use clear coupon names, expiry dates, and minimum order rules. Do not keep expired campaigns active.
Test each coupon from customer cart before advertising. Do not create unlimited high-discount coupons without owner approval.

9. Customers Page

The Customers page helps the owner understand customer history and loyalty.

  • View customer name, mobile, email, order count, lifetime value, and loyalty tier.
  • Mark important customers as VIP where required.
  • Use customer details for support, order follow-up, and WhatsApp communication.
  • Do not expose customer mobile numbers or emails outside business use.

10. Settings Page

Business Settings

  • GST rate
  • Packaging fee
  • Delivery fee and free-delivery threshold
  • Minimum order amount
  • Serviceable PIN codes and delivery radius
  • Kitchen address and coordinates

Operations Settings

  • Store mode: Open, Busy, Paused, Closed
  • Auto-accept order option
  • Decline reason requirement
  • Max orders per slot
  • Default preparation time
  • New order sound and WhatsApp order alerts
During rush hours, use Busy mode instead of closing the website. Use Paused only when the kitchen must temporarily stop receiving orders.

11. Staff Access Page

  • Add staff by name, mobile number, and role.
  • Assign Admin, Manager, or Staff carefully.
  • Deactivate access immediately when a staff member leaves.
  • Review access regularly to keep the admin panel secure.

12. Customer Website Features

Home and Menu

Customers browse categories, search dishes, use filters, see offers, and add items to cart.

Cart

Cart calculates price, GST, delivery, packaging, coupon discount, and free delivery progress.

Checkout

Customers enter receiver details, address, note, and choose COD or online payment when enabled.

Order Tracking

Customers see live order status from placed to delivered or cancelled.

Account and Orders

Customers can log in, view profile, saved addresses, orders, loyalty, and reorder.

Policies

Privacy, terms, refund, delivery, and data deletion pages are included.
